DreamGTM vs Breaking B2B: Which GTM Approach Fits Your Business?

Two senior-led ways to grow a B2B revenue engine. Breaking B2B is a specialist SEO, GEO and AEO agency — technical SEO, content, link building, conversion and AI citations — aimed at pipeline from organic search. DreamGTM is an AI GTM agency: experienced operators who diagnose your whole go-to-market, then build and run it, from positioning through demand, pipeline and AI.

Overview

Two Specialisms, One Goal

Both are expert-led services aimed at the same buyer — B2B and SaaS companies that need pipeline, not impressions. They simply start at different points. One begins with the channel you own and compounds it deeply; the other begins with the system that decides whether any channel works. Here is each one in its own words first — the comparison comes after.

language Breaking B2B

What Is Breaking B2B?

Breaking B2B is a senior-led SEO, GEO and AEO agency for B2B and SaaS companies. Its premise is that your website and the answers buyers read are the channel you own, and that most of that channel is being left unworked — so the job is to rank for the searches your buyers run, get cited in the AI answers they read, and turn both into demos.

Every engagement blends six published capabilities: technical SEO for crawl, render and index problems; SEO content and strategy built from your real sales calls; link building and digital PR into the sources buyers and AI engines trust; conversion and site structure; Reddit marketing in the threads buyers read; and YouTube marketing for bottom-of-funnel video. AEO, GEO and LLM SEO run on top of that foundation. The team is senior SEOs, writers and PR specialists — no junior hand-offs — on six or twelve month programmes.

Its strengths are depth and consistency on one acquisition channel, delivered by people who only work in that channel. It fits teams whose product and positioning are already settled and whose constraint is organic and AI-search visibility, authority and conversion.

account_tree DreamGTM

What Is DreamGTM?

DreamGTM is an expert-led, AI-accelerated GTM system for B2B companies. It connects positioning, demand, pipeline, AI and execution into one operating model, so growth is built as a system rather than assembled from isolated campaigns.

The method runs as five stages — Discover, Design, Execute, Optimize, Scale — and the diagnosis happens inside the same system that later executes it. Six named build systems sit underneath: Discovery Sprint™, Agentic Content OS™, Demand Flow™, Pipeline Acceleration™, AI-Native GTM™ and Growth Lab™. AI compresses the time between a decision and shipped work; it does not replace the operators making the decision.

Its strengths are breadth and accountability across the revenue motion, with experienced people doing the work. It fits teams whose constraint is the system itself — unclear positioning, marketing and sales misalignment, or pipeline that will not predict.

Key Differences

Where the Two Models Diverge

Three structural boundaries explain most of the difference between them. Each is described the way the company concerned would describe it — and where one side does not publish a capability, that is stated as a scope boundary rather than treated as a weakness.

01

One channel versus the whole system

Breaking B2B’s published scope is organic and AI search: technical SEO, content, link building, conversion and site structure, Reddit and YouTube, and getting cited in ChatGPT, Perplexity and AI Overviews. That focus is the product, and it is applied with real depth — a senior specialist team, not a generalist pod.

DreamGTM’s scope is the wider go-to-market — positioning and messaging, content, demand generation, pipeline, sales enablement, AI infrastructure and experimentation — sequenced against each other rather than run in parallel. The two overlap where it matters most, in content, SEO and AEO. The difference is what happens on either side of that work.

02

A channel specialist versus a whole-system operator

Breaking B2B is deliberately narrow in who it serves: B2B and SaaS only, because that is how it already knows the buyer, the cycle and the keywords that convert. Engagements run as six or twelve month programmes on a single 90-day revenue plan, executed by senior SEOs, writers and PR specialists with no junior hand-off.

DreamGTM is deliberately broad. Experienced operators run the diagnosis and then execute against it inside your business, so there is no handover gap between a recommendation and the work — the roadmap is the schedule. It can also be handed to your team at any point to run in-house.

03

Pipeline from one source versus pipeline end to end

Breaking B2B is unusually explicit that it measures leads, demos and revenue rather than organic sessions — but those numbers all trace back to search. The distribution, outbound and enablement motion that turns demand from other sources into pipeline is not part of what it markets.

DreamGTM covers demand through revenue: distribution, partnerships and paid in Demand Flow™, then outbound, sales enablement and conversion in Pipeline Acceleration™, with positioning settled first. That is why pipeline is a deliverable there rather than a reported metric — and why the two are frequently compared at all.

Depth

Where DreamGTM Goes Deeper

Three things that only apply when the scope is the whole system. None of them are claims that the agency is worse at its own job — it is not. They are the reasons a broader engagement exists at all.

01

One accountable owner, not a hand-off between teams

Most GTM problems are not channel problems. They are the seams: positioning set by one team, campaigns run by another, pipeline owned by a third, with nobody holding the sequence. DreamGTM takes one owner across positioning, demand, pipeline and AI — which is what makes the order of operations a decision rather than an accident.

02

Fixes the problem search cannot reach

No amount of ranking rescues a page that targets the wrong buyer, an offer nobody can articulate, or a funnel that leaks before the form. DreamGTM resolves positioning and messaging, demand generation, outbound, enablement and conversion in sequence — the layers that determine whether traffic converts once it finally arrives.

03

Built to be handed over

Everything the diagnosis produces — roadmap, priorities, owners, success metrics — is designed to run without DreamGTM as well as with it. The compounding asset is the system your team learns to operate, not the retainer. Read Discovery Sprint™ to see exactly what that deliverable contains.

FAQ

Common Questions

Direct answers on scope, delivery, AI and which one fits which kind of company.

Is DreamGTM the same as Breaking B2B? expand_more

No. Both are expert-led services sold to B2B and SaaS teams, but they work on different scopes. Breaking B2B is a senior-led SEO, GEO and AEO agency focused on organic search — technical SEO, content, link building, conversion and site structure, plus AI citation work. DreamGTM is an AI-accelerated GTM system that diagnoses the whole go-to-market and then builds and runs positioning, demand, pipeline, AI and execution.

What is the difference between DreamGTM and Breaking B2B? expand_more

The main differences are scope and where the work stops. Breaking B2B runs a disciplined organic search programme — crawl and index fixes, money pages, editorial link acquisition, Reddit and YouTube, and AEO for AI answers — aimed at demos and pipeline from search. DreamGTM covers the wider revenue motion, sequences it against itself, and stays accountable from positioning through closed revenue. They overlap heavily on SEO, AEO and content; the difference is what happens on either side of that work.

Does Breaking B2B handle GTM strategy? expand_more

Partly, and it is worth being precise about where. Breaking B2B starts with a discovery step that learns your ICP, problems, differentiators and goals, then designs a 90-day revenue plan for organic search. That is real strategic work. Its stated scope, however, is search-led pipeline rather than end-to-end GTM strategy across positioning, demand generation channels and the sales motion.

Does DreamGTM provide SEO and AEO? expand_more

Yes, but not as a specialist discipline. SEO and AI answer-engine visibility are handled as part of DreamGTM’s content and AI workstreams rather than as isolated deliverables. If your priority is deep, page-level SEO, AEO, GEO and link acquisition run by senior specialists as the core of the engagement, that is exactly the problem Breaking B2B is built to solve.

Which focuses more on pipeline? expand_more

Both claim pipeline, and Breaking B2B is unusually explicit about it — measuring leads, demos and revenue rather than organic sessions. The distinction is the source of that pipeline. Breaking B2B earns it from one channel: organic search, plus the community and video surfaces that feed it. DreamGTM is accountable for pipeline across demand generation, partnerships, paid, outbound, sales enablement and conversion, with positioning fixed at the top of the sequence.

Which one is more specialist? expand_more

Breaking B2B, clearly. It is B2B and SaaS only, by design, with senior SEOs, writers and PR specialists on the work and no junior hand-offs. It also owns the adjacent surfaces most SEO agencies skip — link building, Reddit threads, YouTube and conversion work. DreamGTM is deliberately broader: the specialism is diagnosing and sequencing the whole revenue system rather than depth in one channel.

Can DreamGTM implement GTM systems? expand_more

Yes. That is the point of running the diagnosis inside an operating system rather than handing over a document. DreamGTM executes across content operations, demand generation, outbound, AI workflows, automation and reporting — and the roadmap can also be taken in-house and run by your own team.

Does Breaking B2B offer more than SEO services? expand_more

More than a typical SEO agency, yes — conversion and site structure, Reddit marketing, YouTube marketing, link building and digital PR are all published capabilities. But every one of them is pointed at the same outcome: organic and AI-search demand. Paid media, outbound, sales enablement and AI workflow automation sit outside what it markets.

Which is better for a B2B SaaS company? expand_more

Both are built for B2B and SaaS, so the deciding question is which layer is constrained. If buyers already know you and the gap is rankings, citations and organic demos, Breaking B2B is the relevant fit. If demand arrives but does not convert, or marketing and sales disagree about the buyer, you need broader GTM work and DreamGTM is the relevant fit.

How should a company choose between them? expand_more

Start by naming the actual constraint rather than the vendor. A single leaky channel points to a channel specialist; unclear positioning, misalignment between marketing and sales, or pipeline that will not predict point to a GTM system. They can also run in sequence, since compounding organic search does not remove the need to fix the wider GTM system.
