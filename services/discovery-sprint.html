The Process

Diagnose → Audit → Identify → Prioritize → Roadmap

Five stages, in order. Nothing gets recommended before it’s been diagnosed, and nothing gets prioritized before the bottlenecks are named. This is the Discover stage of the DreamGTM Method™ — its output is what the Design stage turns into a roadmap.

01

Diagnose

We immerse ourselves in your business: product, buyers, pipeline data, current motion, team and the constraints you’re actually working under.

02

Audit

We run the GTM audit across all eight layers — ICP, positioning, messaging, competitive set, funnel, demand, pipeline and AI infrastructure.

03

Identify

We isolate bottlenecks, working assets and whitespace — and separate surface symptoms from the root causes underneath them.

04

Prioritize

Every initiative gets ranked against impact, effort, confidence and strategic fit — then sorted into stop, fix, test, double down and build.

05

Roadmap

The ranked decisions become a 90-day GTM roadmap with owners, sequencing and success metrics tied to revenue.

Prioritization

Impact × Effort. Then Confidence And Strategic Fit.

Every idea that survives the audit gets placed on impact against effort. Then it’s stress-tested on the two things spreadsheets always miss: how confident we are in the evidence, and whether it reinforces the strategy you’ve chosen.

Five decisions, made explicit

Nothing sits in an undifferentiated “ideas” list.

Stop

Initiatives consuming capacity without evidence they’ll ever move pipeline.

Fix

Broken layers — positioning, funnel, handoff — where repair beats replacement.

Test

Hypotheses worth a limited run before you commit budget or headcount.

Double Down

What already works and is under-invested. Usually the fastest revenue available.

Build

New systems and capabilities the roadmap requires to compound.

90-Day GTM Roadmap

How Findings Become Motion

A diagnosis nobody acts on is wasted spend. The roadmap turns the ranked decisions into Foundation, then Build and Launch, then Optimize — with owners, sequencing and success metrics tied to revenue.

Weeks 1–4

Foundation

Lock the decisions. Fix the leaks that are costing you pipeline today before adding anything new.

ICP and positioning aligned across marketing, sales and product
Funnel bottlenecks fixed at the stages losing the most revenue
Initiatives killed or paused — the Reconsider quadrant, closed out
Owners assigned to every remaining priority

Weeks 5–8

Build & Launch

Ship the strategic bets. Quick wins go live first so the team feels the prioritization was real.

Quick wins shipped and measured
Priority systems built — content, demand, pipeline or AI workflows
Marketing/sales handoff re-aligned to one qualification standard
Limited tests running on the hypotheses worth testing

Weeks 9–12

Optimize

What’s live gets measured and improved. What didn’t work gets stopped and re-ranked.

Success metrics reviewed against revenue impact
Conversion and channel efficiency optimized
Underperformers cut, winners scaled
Next priorities re-ranked for the following cycle

Optimize isn’t a phase that ends at week 12 — it’s the continuous optimization loop of the DreamGTM Method™. The roadmap just gives the first cycle a fixed shape.

FAQ

GTM Discovery Sprint — Questions, Answered

Straight answers about what the sprint is, who it’s for and what happens after.

What is a GTM Discovery Sprint? expand_more

The Discovery Sprint™ is the Discover stage of the DreamGTM Method™ and an expert-led diagnosis of your go-to-market system. We immerse ourselves in your business, audit ICP, positioning, messaging, competitive landscape, funnel, demand, pipeline and AI/GTM infrastructure, identify the bottlenecks, and prioritize the initiatives with the greatest business impact. The output is a ranked set of decisions and a clear 90-day action plan — not a strategy deck.

What does a GTM audit include? expand_more

Eight areas of your GTM system: ICP and market, positioning, messaging, competitive landscape, funnel and conversion, demand generation, pipeline and sales motion, and AI/GTM infrastructure. For each one we establish what is working, what is broken, where the funnel leaks and where there is whitespace worth exploiting.

Who is the Discovery Sprint for? expand_more

B2B SaaS and technology companies — typically founders, and marketing or revenue leaders — that have GTM activity without a clear system: unclear ICP or positioning, weak marketing/sales alignment, too many competing growth initiatives, inconsistent pipeline, funnel bottlenecks, unclear channel priorities, or growth activity that nobody owns. If you already know exactly what is broken and only need it executed, a diagnostic sprint is not the right first step.

What do we receive at the end of a Discovery Sprint? expand_more

An ICP assessment, a positioning and messaging diagnosis, a competitive review, a funnel and bottleneck analysis, an opportunity map, a prioritized list of initiatives, and a 90-day GTM roadmap built on those findings with clear priorities, success metrics, owners and expected outcomes. We close with a leadership readout and agreed next steps.

How is this different from GTM strategy consulting? expand_more

Consulting typically hands over a strategy document and leaves execution to you. The Discovery Sprint™ is diagnostic and decision-led: it identifies what is holding growth back, prioritizes it against your effort, confidence and strategic-fit constraints, and produces a plan built to be executed. DreamGTM then runs the same system end to end — Discover, Design, Execute, Optimize, Scale — so strategy and execution stay connected instead of living in separate documents.

How long does a Discovery Sprint take? expand_more

The sprint runs as the Discover stage of the DreamGTM Method™ and produces the inputs for a 90-day GTM roadmap. Scope, access and the calendar are agreed with your team before work starts, so the schedule is set around your operating rhythm rather than a fixed template.

What happens after the Discovery Sprint? expand_more

The findings become the 90-day GTM roadmap: Foundation, then Build and Launch, then Optimize. From there, execution happens inside the DreamGTM system that matches your diagnosis — Agentic Content OS™, Demand Flow™, Pipeline Acceleration™, AI-Native GTM™ or Growth Lab™. The roadmap is designed to be executable by your team or by ours.

Can DreamGTM implement the recommendations? expand_more

Yes. That is the point of running the diagnosis inside a GTM operating system rather than handing you a document. DreamGTM executes across content operations, demand generation, outbound, AI workflows, automation and reporting, with end-to-end pipeline accountability — and you can also take the roadmap in-house.
