The Problem

Pipeline Problems Are Usually System Problems.

The activity is there. The systems are not connected: targeting is blunt, buying signals are missed, qualification is inconsistent, follow-up is slow, and marketing hands work to sales without context. Each of those leaks looks small on its own and compounds into a forecast nobody trusts.

Where the funnel is supposed to narrow

Every stage should lose volume and gain quality. The gate below each stage is what has to be true for that to happen — which is where most pipelines actually leak.

01

Signals detected

Accounts showing evaluation behaviour inside the target base.

The gate Is this signal attached to a named account, with an owner, instead of sitting in a dashboard?

02

Prioritized

Fit and intent combined into a working order the team can act on.

The gate Can anyone explain why an account ranked where it did — and change the ranking when reality disagrees?

03

Qualified

Accounts that clear a written bar, judged the same way every time.

The gate Is the bar written down, evidenced, and applied by everyone working the same funnel?

04

Conversations

Engaged accounts with a real buying conversation happening.

The gate Was the right person in the room, with context, fast enough to keep the interest alive?

05

Opportunities

Deals with agreed next steps, an owner and a stage you would defend.

The gate Is every stalled deal diagnosed by name, or is it simply the same quiet slide every quarter?

06

Revenue

Sourced and influenced pipeline traced back to what produced it.

The gate Can marketing and sales agree the definition of sourced pipeline, and read the same report?

The bar widths describe where a funnel is meant to narrow, not measured volumes. The real ratios come from your funnel and are agreed during the diagnosis — we do not set them, and we do not promise a number.

FAQ

Pipeline Acceleration — Questions, Answered

Straight answers on what Pipeline Acceleration™ is, how it works with your sales team, and how it is measured.

What is Pipeline Acceleration? expand_more

Pipeline Acceleration™ is the system that turns the GTM signals, target accounts, intent and sales activity a company already has into more qualified pipeline and better conversion between funnel stages. It connects account prioritization, buying signals, messaging, outreach, qualification, routing and sales workflows as one operating system, so the goal is pipeline quality and stage conversion rather than simply more leads.

How is it different from lead generation? expand_more

Lead generation optimises for captured contacts — form fills, MQLs, booked meetings. Pipeline Acceleration optimises for what happens after the contact exists: whether the account was worth targeting, whether the message matched the buyer, whether follow-up happened fast enough, and whether the handoff into sales carried context. We do not sell lead volume, and volume commitments are not part of this engagement.

Does it include outbound? expand_more

Yes — outbound is one layer inside the system, not the whole offer. Pipeline Acceleration™ covers target account selection, signal detection, messaging and personalization, outbound and inbound workflows, qualification, lead routing and sales handoffs, nurture, and CRM and GTM automation. The mix is chosen against your ICP and buying cycle. Scope, volume and cadence are agreed with your team before anything launches, and we do not commit to a fixed number of sends or meetings.

How do you identify high-intent accounts? expand_more

Two sources, read together. First the fit layer: firmographics, segment and role characteristics that define who can actually buy. Second the intent layer: the behaviour that says an account is in-market now — pricing and documentation activity, comparison and category research, hiring and product-evaluation signals, technology changes, and how often a named account returns. Both are written down as prioritization logic, so your team can see why an account ranked where it did.

How does AI fit into the system? expand_more

AI is the execution layer, not the strategy. It absorbs the volume work — account research, signal detection, message personalization, lead and account qualification, workflow automation, follow-ups and sales intelligence — so operators spend their time on targeting, messaging and the decisions that change the outcome. It does not set the ICP, approve the messaging, or decide which accounts get an executive’s attention. Those stay with the people running the system.

Does it work with an existing sales team? expand_more

Yes. In most engagements the sales team already exists and is the part doing the conversion. The work is to make the handoff qualified and the context readable: qualification logic, routing rules, follow-up sequences, the sales handoff framework and pipeline reporting in your existing CRM. We define the handoff; your reps work it. Where a team has no defined process at all, that is usually diagnosed first in the Discovery Sprint™.

What does implementation involve? expand_more

It runs on the DreamGTM Method™ — Discover, Design, Execute, Optimize, Scale. Discover reads your funnel, data and current motion. Design produces the target account framework, qualification logic, signal strategy and pipeline workflow. Execute builds the messaging, outreach sequences, routing, handoffs and automations. Optimize and Scale run on the reporting and the optimization plan. Timelines and scope are agreed with your team, and we do not publish a standard duration before the diagnosis.

How is success measured? expand_more

Stage by stage, against the funnel you already have. Coverage: how much of the target account base is actually being worked. Qualification: how many accounts clear the bar that is written down. Conversion: MQL to SQL to opportunity to closed, and where the drop happens. Responsiveness: the speed of follow-up and handoff. Commercial: sourced and influenced pipeline traced back to the segment, message and source that produced it. Definitions are agreed up front so the number means the same thing to marketing and sales.

What happens after launch? expand_more

The system keeps running and keeps learning. Stage signals feed the next cycle, the optimization plan re-ranks what gets attention, and the workflow layer is documented and handed over rather than held back. Work that moves beyond pipeline conversion joins the rest of the DreamGTM Method™: Agentic Content OS™ and Demand Flow™ upstream, AI-Native GTM™ automating the workflow layer underneath, and Growth Lab™ for testing what to scale next.
