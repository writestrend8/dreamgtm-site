How Demand Flow Works

Define → Create → Distribute → Capture → Nurture → Learn → Optimize

Seven stages, one system. Each stage has an owner, an output and a signal, and each output is the next stage’s input. Nothing here runs as a standalone campaign.

01

Define

The audience, the category claim and the difference you defend — resolved before a rupee or a dollar goes anywhere.

Out: one ICP, one message

02

Create

The assets that carry the argument — produced with your experts and accelerated through the workflow layer.

Out: message-carrying assets

03

Distribute

Organic, LinkedIn, paid and partners. Each asset ships with named surfaces, targeting and an owner — never “publish and hope”.

Out: qualified attention

04

Capture

Signals, forms and enrichment recorded against the account and the stage — so what we know about a buyer is usable, not just stored.

Out: readable buyer context

05

Nurture

Sequences and retargeting matched to where the buyer actually is, so attention keeps working between buying cycles.

Out: stage progression

06

Learn

Stage signals, sales feedback and win/loss input read together, so we know which message, segment and surface actually moved a buyer.

Out: evidence, not opinion

07

Optimize

Budget, targeting and creative re-ranked on what the evidence supports. What underperforms is stopped; what works gets more.

Out: a stronger next cycle

repeat

Optimize is not a phase that ends — it is the continuous loop the DreamGTM Method™ runs on. Learn feeds back into Define, so each cycle starts from evidence about your market instead of a fresh guess.

FAQ

B2B Demand Generation — Questions, Answered

Straight answers on what Demand Flow™ is, how it works with your channels, and how it is measured.

What is B2B demand generation? expand_more

B2B demand generation is the work of making a defined set of buyers aware that you exist, clear on why you matter to them, and actively looking at you — before anyone fills in a form or books a call. In Demand Flow™ it runs as a system: the audience is defined once, the message is built for that buyer, content and distribution carry it, and nurture keeps it moving until the buyer is ready for a conversation. Capture is a step inside that system, not the goal of it.

How is Demand Flow different from lead generation? expand_more

Lead generation optimises for captured contact — form fills, MQLs, booked meetings. Demand Flow optimises for familiarity, relevance and intent across the buyer journey. The buyer should reach the capture step already knowing what you do and why it applies to them, and should keep moving between cycles. That is why we measure the signals in between — reach, engagement depth, high-intent actions, stage progression — instead of only the volume at the end.

Which channels are included? expand_more

Demand Flow™ covers the layers we build and run: organic and SEO demand, LinkedIn and paid distribution, content and thought leadership, audience targeting, partnerships and distribution surfaces, buyer signal capture, and nurture and retargeting. The mix is chosen against your ICP, your category and your buying cycle — not copied from someone else’s playbook. Scope, spend and cadence are agreed with your team before anything launches.

How does Demand Flow work with SEO and content? expand_more

Content is treated as the demand layer it is, not as an SEO deliverable. Topic architecture, assets and internal linking come from the Agentic Content OS™ side of the system and hand off into Demand Flow™, where distribution, paid and nurture decide who sees them and what happens next. Organic demand is measured the same way paid is — by reach to the right buyer, engagement depth and the pipeline it contributes to. Where organic is not the constraint, we will tell you before building it.

How does Demand Flow work with sales? expand_more

Marketing and sales read the same signals. What a named account did, which stage it reached and how often it came back is visible to both, so a conversation starts from context rather than a form submission. When the work stops being demand creation and becomes conversion, that piece moves into Pipeline Acceleration™. We do not claim the closed meeting — we make sure the handoff is qualified.

How does AI improve demand generation? expand_more

AI is leverage inside the system, not the product. It accelerates the volume work — research, audience intelligence, content production, repurposing, personalization, distribution workflows, signal analysis and testing — so operators spend their time on the argument, the targeting and the budget decisions rather than the assembly. It does not decide the audience, approve the message or invent the proof. Those stay with the people running the demand system.

How do you measure demand generation? expand_more

Stage by stage, against the audience you defined. Awareness: visibility and qualified reach to the target segment. Engagement: depth and return behaviour. Intent: the actions that signal evaluation — pricing, docs, comparison, demo requests. Nurture: stage progression between cycles. Commercial: sourced and influenced pipeline traced back to the segment, message and channel that produced it. Reporting shows contribution, not impressions.

How is Demand Flow different from Pipeline Acceleration™? expand_more

They sit either side of the same handoff. Demand Flow™ creates, captures and nurtures demand: it builds familiarity, relevance and intent with the market. Pipeline Acceleration™ converts qualified demand and intent into pipeline through outbound, sales enablement and conversion work. If demand is not your constraint, Pipeline Acceleration™ is the right place to start — the Discovery Sprint™ is where that gets diagnosed.

What happens after Demand Flow is implemented? expand_more

The system keeps running and keeps learning. Stage signals feed the next cycle, the optimization loop re-ranks what gets budget, and as more demand becomes qualified that work moves into Pipeline Acceleration™ while AI-Native GTM™ keeps automating the workflow layer underneath it. Demand Flow™ is built to be run by your team as well as ours — the operating logic is documented, not held back.
